MyRide

Delete your MyRide account

In the app

  1. Open MyRide and tap your profile (top left), then Account.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Finish or cancel any current or scheduled trip first.

What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email and business details.
  • Your saved contacts for trip sharing, chat messages and notification tokens.
  • You are signed out on every phone. MyRide Plus stops renewing.

What is kept

Trip and payment records are kept as the law requires for accounting, tax and safety, without your name, number, notes, chats or shared contacts. Unused credits are lost.

Riders (MyRide Drive)

Rider accounts include payouts and verification records, so they are closed by MyRide support: contact support from the app.

See also our privacy policy.
