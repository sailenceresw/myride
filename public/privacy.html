MyRide

Privacy policy

For the MyRide app (customers) and MyRide Drive (riders). Last updated 23 October 2026.

What we collect, and why

  • Mobile number and name: to sign you in (by SMS code) and so your rider or customer knows who to look for.
  • Location: in MyRide, while you use the app, to set your pickup and show nearby vehicles. In MyRide Drive, while you are online or on a trip (also in the background during a trip), to send you trips and to show the customer where you are.
  • Trips: pickup, stops, destination, route, times, fare, payment method, ratings and comments: to run the trip, give you receipts, handle complaints and meet legal and tax duties.
  • Chat messages you send during a trip: delivered to the other person on that trip.
  • People you share a trip with: the names and numbers you choose. We text them your live trip link, then again when you arrive or press SOS. We never contact them otherwise.
  • Payments: when you pay by eSewa or Khalti, the payment happens on their page; we keep the transaction reference and amount, never your wallet password or PIN.
  • Riders (MyRide Drive): identity and vehicle documents, licence, bank account for payouts and selfie checks, to verify riders and keep customers safe.
  • Device: a push notification token, so we can tell you about your trip.

Who can see what

  • Your rider sees your first name, pickup, stops, destination and any note you add. Your customer sees the rider's name, photo, vehicle, plate and live location during the trip.
  • Phone numbers are not shown in trip details, and calls are only possible during a trip. Where MyRide's call line is available, customers and riders are connected through it without seeing each other's number; otherwise the call is placed directly and the number appears in your phone's call log for that call.
  • A trip link you share shows the live trip and the rider's first name and plate, never phone numbers.
  • MyRide operations staff can see trips and accounts to provide support, handle safety reports (SOS) and pay riders.
  • We use service providers to send SMS, push notifications, maps and routing, and to process payments. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you use MyRide. Trip and payment records are kept as long as the law requires for accounting, tax and safety, even after you delete your account, but without your name, number, notes, chats or shared contacts.

Your choices

  • Turn off location permission at any time in your phone's settings (you will then choose your pickup on the map).
  • Change your name in Account. Remove saved places or shared contacts.
  • Delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account), or see how to delete your account.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: contact MyRide support from the app (Account → Help and support).
